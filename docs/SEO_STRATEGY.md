# WayToMoksha SEO Strategy

## Purpose

This document defines the SEO strategy for WayToMoksha.org.

The goal is to establish WayToMoksha.org as the authoritative online source for the official WayToMoksha organization, its mission, daily sessions, retreats, donations, volunteering, and future services.

## SEO Problem Statement

Current search visibility is weak.

When users search for WayToMoksha-related keywords, results may show:

- YouTube results
- Social media results
- Other websites using similar names
- Similar domains such as Way2Moksha
- Partial or outdated WayToMoksha pages

The official website should become the trusted destination for users, followers, volunteers, donors, retreat participants, and administrators.

## Primary SEO Goal

Make WayToMoksha.org rank strongly for:

- WayToMoksha
- Way To Moksha
- Waytomoksha
- Way to Moksha
- WayToMoksha.org
- Way To Moksha Foundation
- Way To Moksha Foundation Inc
- WayToMoksha meditation

- WayToMoksha donations
- WayToMoksha volunteer

## Brand Authority Goal

The website should clearly communicate that:

- WayToMoksha.org is the official website
- Way To Moksha FOUNDATION INC is the official organization
- The organization is a non-profit and 501(c)(3) tax-exempt organization in the USA

## Technical SEO Checklist

- robots.txt

## Search Console Setup

After deployment:

1. Add WayToMoksha.org to Google Search Console.
2. Verify domain ownership.
3. Submit sitemap.xml.
4. Request indexing for key pages.
5. Monitor search queries.
6. Monitor indexing issues.
7. Monitor brand confusion queries.
8. Repeat with Bing Webmaster Tools.

## Brand Confusion / Similar Domain Monitoring

Track domains or pages that may confuse users searching for WayToMoksha.

Known concern:

- way2moksha.org

Actions:

- Capture screenshots of confusing search results.
- Record dates and search queries.
- Compare branding, naming, keywords, and content.
- Gather official WayToMoksha organization evidence.
- Consult an IP/domain attorney before any dispute action.
- Consider UDRP only if trademark/common-law rights, confusing similarity, lack of legitimate interest, and bad-faith registration/use can be supported.

## Domain Dispute Research

Potential path:

- Trademark review
- Common-law trademark evidence collection
- WHOIS/domain registration research
- Confusion evidence
- Attorney review
- Cease-and-desist option
- UDRP option
- Court option if needed

Do not make legal claims on the website.
