# WayToMoksha Requirements

## Current Scope

This document focuses on Project 1: the public website.

The website should be simple, clean, content-driven, and extendable.

## Main Navigation

The website should have the following main tabs:

1. Home
2. About
3. Daily Sessions
4. Retreats
5. Volunteer
6. Donate
7. Contact

Do not add too many top-level tabs. Seven is acceptable because each tab represents a clear user intent.

## About Page Requirements

The About page should explain:

- What WayToMoksha is
- Mission
- Vision
- Guided journey / getting started path
- Why users may feel confused today
- How the website helps them follow a clear path

Known About content:

WayToMoksha is a non-religious, non-profit voluntary organization whose sole mission is to spread mindful meditation, vegetarianism, and to make everyone aware of their inner self to enlighten themselves. We believe that by practicing mindful meditation and vegetarianism, people can become more connected to themselves and the world around them. Our organization is dedicated to helping people achieve this connection and enlightenment.

We believe in illuminating lives with dreams, meditation, spirituality, and growth.

Mission:

Our mission is to inspire and guide individuals on a transformative journey of self-discovery, inner growth, and spiritual awakening. We are dedicated to creating a nurturing space where people can explore the profound depths of their consciousness, harness the power of their dreams, and connect with their inner wisdom to lead a more purposeful and fulfilling life.

Vision:

Our vision is to create a world where individuals awaken to their fullest potential, embracing the power of dreams, meditation, and spirituality to lead purpose-driven, balanced, and harmonious lives.

- Name

- Phone

- Preferred language
- Areas of interest
- Availability
- Notes or motivation

Volunteer interest areas may include:

- General volunteering
- Retreat support
- Zoom/session support
- Video editing
