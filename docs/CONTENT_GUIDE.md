# WayToMoksha Content Guide

## Content Sources

Content comes from three sources:

1. Current WayToMoksha website
2. WhatsApp community screenshots and messages
3. Approved design/product images created by volunteers

The current website should be treated as a reference, not a final source of truth.

Some content on the current website may come from a template and should not be reused unless it clearly belongs to WayToMoksha.

## Content Strategy

Use real WayToMoksha content wherever available.

If content is missing:

- Use simple placeholders
- Mark as needing admin review
- Do not invent claims
- Do not overstate features

## Tone

The tone should be:

- Calm
- Respectful
- Clear
- Spiritual but non-religious
- Trustworthy
- Simple enough for global users

Avoid:

- Overly corporate wording
- Template-style marketing language
- Heavy spiritual jargon without explanation
- Confusing long paragraphs

WayToMoksha is a non-religious, non-profit voluntary organization whose sole mission is to spread mindful meditation, vegetarianism, and to make everyone aware of their inner self to enlighten themselves. We believe that by practicing mindful meditation and vegetarianism, people can become more connected to themselves and the world around them. Our organization is dedicated to helping people achieve this connection and enlightenment.

We believe in illuminating lives with dreams, meditation, spirituality, and growth.

Our mission is to inspire and guide individuals on a transformative journey of self-discovery, inner growth, and spiritual awakening.
